About

Daniel loves creating useful and fun things, exploring ideas, making sense of complexity. He’s a purpose-driven generalist, who works to see human potential actualized, envisioning a world where technology elevates the best of our nature.

Douglas Engelbart, Ivan Sutherland, Bret Victor, Tristan Harris and Mike Matas are some of his heroes.

When not at his desk you'll find him out in nature, with family and friends, in the kitchen, in a swimming pool, or in a cinema.
