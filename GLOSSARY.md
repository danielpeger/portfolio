# Portfolio

Daniel Péger's project write-ups and the media that belongs to them.

## Language

**Article**:
The project write-up. The picture above an article stands outside it.
_Avoid_: Page, case study, post

**Hero**:
The picture above an article. It stands outside the article.
_Avoid_: Cover, thumbnail, banner

**Lightbox set**:
The hero and the images and videos in one article that open together and cycle as one sequence. Any member can be left out.
_Avoid_: Gallery, album

**Slide**:
One image or video in a lightbox set.
_Avoid_: Item, frame, photo

**Caption**:
Text that belongs to a slide. A slide may have none.
_Avoid_: Label, description
